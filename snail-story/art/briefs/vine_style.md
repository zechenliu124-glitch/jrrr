## Grapevine style (identical in the spring and summer vine files)
A realistic old grapevine — twisted, gnarled woody trunk with fibrous, shaggy bark in long vertical strips,
knots and small scars; light from the upper left (lighter left edge, darker right edge, soft core shadow), palette
'Vine wood / bark'. Branches (canes) thinner and smoother, warmer brown-grey. Real grape leaves: palmate,
5-lobed, toothed edge, visible palmate veins, petiole; spring leaves small, light, slightly translucent (palette
'Spring leaves'); summer leaves large, mature (palette 'Summer leaves'). Curly tendrils. Painted with wc-paint
(bark/leaves grouped), wc-line for veins and bark strokes. Clean, not cluttered.
